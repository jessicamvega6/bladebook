Rinkside Account
(Written 2026-10-12 from the Harbor design: design/harbor/rinkside/static/account.html)

The Account tab of the rinkside app. The rest of the app is described in Coach App.

Shows:
  The coach's name and login email.
  Rink: the rink the coach is at.
  Last synced: when the phone last got today's lessons, with "no signal now" when offline.
  Waiting to send: how many check-ins are saved on the phone and not sent yet, or "Nothing".

"Everything else is on the website": the schedule, students, approvals, payments and profile are on the
coach website. ~~This app only sees today's lessons, attendance and cancelling.~~ *This page is for today's lessons, attendance and
cancelling. (Owner, 2026-11-09, R9: the page signs in like the website and can reach everything it can, so the text
says what the page is for, not what it can see.)*

Log out button. If check-ins are still waiting to send, logging out warns first (see C_Rinkside Offline.md).
